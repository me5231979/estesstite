01 / Color

Dark by default. Ember is a signal.

Ember covers 5% of a screen at most. One ember element per viewport is the target, two is the ceiling.

--ink#14110F · Page
--surface#1E1A17 · Cards
--raised#2A2420 · Hover
--line#3A322C · Borders
--bone#F2EDE4 · Text
--ash#A89F94 · Captions
--ember#E8652B · Accent
--amber#F2A541 · Highlight

Measured contrast: bone on ink 16.1:1. Ash on ink 7.2:1. Ember on ink 5.7:1. Ember on raised 4.6:1. Ink on ember 5.7:1.
