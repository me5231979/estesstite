Privacy

Privacy, in plain words.

This site sets no cookies, runs no tracking or analytics, and collects nothing unless you choose to write to me.

Last updated 24 October 2026 Have counsel review before relying on this

Who is responsible

Matt Estes runs this site and is responsible for any personal data it handles. Write to hello@mattestes.com with any question or request.

What this site collects

Nothing, by itself. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no third-party scripts. Fonts and media load from this site, not from outside services.

The contact forms don't send anything to a server. They open your own email app with a draft. If you send it, I receive your email address and what you wrote, the same as any email.

The Capability Diagnostic runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are never sent or stored.

What stays on your device

If you turn sound on, your browser remembers that choice in local storage on your own device. You asked for it, it never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages and may also be served by Vercel. Like any web host, they record technical data such as IP addresses in server logs to deliver and protect the site. Their own privacy policies cover that processing.

Emails you send me

I use them only to reply and to follow up on the conversation you started. I don't sell or share them, and I don't add you to a mailing list. I delete them when they're no longer needed. You can ask me at any time to see, correct, or delete what I hold about you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EU, UK, and California, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email me and I'll respond within 30 days.

Links to other sites

Links to LinkedIn and other sites take you to services with their own privacy practices. Some resource pages on this domain embed outside services, such as Microsoft Forms, Spotify, and Google Fonts, which may set their own cookies.
