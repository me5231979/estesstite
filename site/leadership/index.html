Leadership

How I lead.

5 rules I run my team by.

  1. Small team, big surface.

    I run a 3-person function serving 4,600 staff. That forces clarity.

  2. I build the first version myself.

    Then the team owns it. Nobody waits on me twice.

  3. Decisions go in writing.

    If it isn't on a page, it isn't decided.

  4. I tell leaders what the data says.

    Not what they want to hear. Then I bring the fix.

  5. I hire for judgment.

    Then I get out of the way.
