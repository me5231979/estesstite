Essay 01

The end of L&D as we know it

Most learning functions are publishers. They produce courses, track completions, and report activity. Leadership nods. Nothing changes.

That model was built for a world where skills held still. They don't anymore. The World Economic Forum projects that 39% of core skills will change by 2030. Most L&D strategies plan 1 to 2 years out.

That gap is the whole story.

The fix isn't better courses. It's a different job.

A capability team does 4 things. It finds where the work is changing. It builds just enough learning to close the gap. It puts that learning inside the work, not beside it. Then it checks whether behavior moved, and does it again next quarter.

That means fewer programs and more tools. Fewer catalogs and more diagnostics. Less content and more practice. It means L&D leaders who can read a P&L, build a prototype, and tell an executive the program they love isn't working.

Learning isn't the product. Capability is.

The functions that get this will become the most important team in the building. The ones that don't will be replaced by a subscription.

Source: World Economic Forum, Future of Jobs Report 2025.
